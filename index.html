The idea

More AI is not the goal.

Some jobs are best done by people. Some are best with AI doing the first pass. Some should run on their own. Which is which depends on the job, the industry and the company. We call the right point the sweet spot, and we find it from what works for companies like yours.

Too little

Work a machine could carry is still done by hand.

The sweet spot

The way of working that goes with the best results.

Too much

AI where it costs quality, trust or money.

What we ask

We ask what happened.

No ratings from 1 to 5. No right answers. If you do not know, you can say so.

  1. How the work gets done

    Job by job: by hand, in a tool, with AI helping, or run by AI. And whether that is the right amount.

  2. A few facts

    The last campaign. Last quarter's numbers. Things that happened.

  3. The data behind it

    What feeds the work, and how your systems connect.

  4. The tools

    What you use, whether you would choose it again, and what is about to change. Asked last.

What your company gets

One picture of how your marketing works.

  • Built from the answers of everyone who took part.
  • Where you stand among companies like yours, job by job.
  • Where you are under your sweet spot, in it, or past it.
  • What the teams with the best results do differently.
  • Your tools, next to what comparable teams use.
Your answers

Nobody sees who said what.

  • Your company sees one combined picture, never individual answers.
  • Other companies see totals only. No company is ever named.
  • Makers of marketing tools can buy totals across many companies. They never see anything about yours.
  • Every group we report has at least eight companies in it.
